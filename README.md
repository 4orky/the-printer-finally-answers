The office printer finally talks back. One line, in the machine's own voice — it ate your spreadsheet, it was the highlight of its week, and it would do it again. The program writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`:

    Paper jam. I ate your spreadsheet, and it was the most interesting thing I've printed all week. I'd do it again.

## How the line was picked

Fourteen one-liners were drafted by hand against the brief and graded on laugh, voice and snap, weighted 3–2–1 so humour counts most. The highest grade wins; a tie goes to the shorter line. The winning draft then went through a real second pass: the filler word "honestly" was cut and the dare "I'd do it again." was added, so the comeback lands on the human instead of trailing off. The full drafts, grades and revision note live in the header of `main.py`.

## Try it

    python3 ./main.py

The run prints the line with its character count and SHA-256, and leaves `/output/entry.txt` holding exactly those bytes — verified by re-reading the file before the program exits.
